Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Mutual Small Business Consulting ("we", "us") provides AI reception services to businesses in Ontario. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and how we protect it, in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Information we collect

  • From businesses that contact us: name, business name, type of business, service area, email address and, if provided, phone number.
  • From callers to our clients' lines: when our AI receptionist answers a call for a client business, it may collect the caller's name, phone number, address and the reason for the call, and may record and transcribe the call. Callers are told they are speaking with an automated assistant.
  • From our website: basic technical information such as browser type, used only to keep the site working.

How we use it

  • To set up and run the reception service, and to pass messages and bookings to the client business.
  • To respond to demo requests and questions, and to send service and billing emails.
  • To improve the accuracy of the receptionist for that client.

We do not sell personal information. We send marketing emails only where Canada's anti-spam law allows it, and every message includes a way to unsubscribe.

Who handles caller information

For calls answered on a client's behalf, the client business controls the caller information, and we handle it only to provide the service. We use trusted third-party providers for telephone, speech, AI and email services. Some of these providers may store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to local laws.

How long we keep it

We keep call records and transcripts only as long as needed to provide the service to the client, or as the client instructs, and delete them when the client's service ends unless the law requires otherwise.

How we protect it

We limit access to people and systems that need it, use providers with reasonable security safeguards, and use encrypted connections.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or unsubscribe from our emails at any time. If you called one of our client businesses, you may also contact that business directly.
