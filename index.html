Questions owners ask

Do I need to change my phone number?

No. You keep your number and forward missed or after-hours calls to your receptionist. You can switch it off any time.

Will callers know it's not a person?

Yes. It tells callers it's an automated assistant. Most people just want their problem booked quickly, and it does that.

What if a caller asks something it doesn't know?

It takes their details and tells them you'll call back. It only shares answers you've approved.

Is there a contract?

Month to month. Cancel any month with no penalty.
